# Northwind Analytics — Annual Report 2025

## Revenue

Total revenue for the financial year reached $48.2 million, an increase of
twenty-two percent on the prior year. Growth was concentrated in the enterprise
segment, where annual recurring revenue rose from $19.4 million to $27.1
million. Mid-market revenue was broadly flat at $14.6 million, and the
self-serve tier declined slightly to $6.5 million as customers migrated upward.

Net revenue retention across the enterprise base was 118 percent. Gross
retention was 94 percent, with churn concentrated among customers acquired
during the 2023 promotional pricing period.

## Costs and margin

Gross margin improved from 71 percent to 76 percent, driven mainly by the
migration of the data pipeline off managed services and onto reserved capacity.
Infrastructure spend fell by $2.1 million year over year despite a forty percent
increase in processed volume.

Operating expenses rose to $31.8 million. Sales and marketing accounted for
$14.2 million of that, research and development $12.4 million, and general and
administrative $5.2 million.

## Outlook

Management expects revenue growth to moderate to between twelve and fifteen
percent in the coming year as the enterprise segment matures. Two risks are
called out: concentration, with the ten largest customers representing 34
percent of revenue, and the renewal cliff in the third quarter, when contracts
worth $8.9 million come up together.
