# Customer Satisfaction — Q4 Review

## Headline numbers

Net Promoter Score finished the quarter at 41, up from 33 in Q3 and 28 a year
ago. Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) on support interactions averaged 4.4 out of 5
across 3,812 rated conversations.

Median first-response time fell to 47 minutes from 2 hours 20 minutes, following
the move to a follow-the-sun rota across the London and Vancouver teams.

## What customers praised

Reliability was the most frequently cited strength, appearing in 61 percent of
positive free-text responses. The redesigned onboarding flow was second, cited
in 34 percent. Several enterprise accounts specifically named the dedicated
migration support offered during contract renewal.

## What customers complained about

Reporting flexibility remains the largest source of dissatisfaction, named in 44
percent of negative responses. Customers want to build their own views rather
than choose from fixed dashboards. Export limits were the second complaint, at
27 percent.

Pricing transparency appeared in 19 percent of negative responses, almost
entirely from mid-market accounts moving between tiers.
